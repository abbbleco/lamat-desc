LAMAT | AI Social Dating App With Admin Panel

Lamat is a complete dating and social community platform built with Laravel and Flutter — with AI enhancing the moments where it helps most. Launch your own modern dating app with swiping, real-time chat, voice and video calls, stories, reels, live streaming, dating games, and a full monetization suite — all managed from a powerful admin panel with 150+ ready REST API endpoints.

Warning: Third-party services like Google Gemini AI, Pusher, Giphy, Zego Cloud, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), OneSignal, Google Geolocation API, Google Maps API, Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay, Mercado Pago, Mollie, Flutterwave, Sight Engine, Google OAuth, Apple OAuth, Facebook OAuth, SMTP Email Service, and Google Analytics require separate paid subscriptions or API costs not covered by the purchase price.

Why Lamat

Lamat combines a full dating app, a social network, and a live-streaming platform in one package. Members can swipe, chat, call, post stories and reels, go live, join voice rooms, and play dating games — with AI quietly helping with translation, replies, and matching — while you earn from coins, subscriptions, gifts, pay-per-view content, and withdrawals. Everything is controlled from the admin panel with no coding required.

Discovery, Matching & Profiles

Give members a familiar swipe experience with profile cards, mutual-match detection, and instant notifications. Location, age, interest, and preference filters keep discovery relevant, while configurable free limits and boosts create a natural path to paid plans. Rich profiles support photos, intro videos, interests, Q&A, verifications, rankings, and privacy controls such as incognito mode.

Chat, Voice & Video Calls

Members stay in conversation with instant one-to-one messaging — text, photos, video, voice notes, GIFs, stickers, replies, forwards, and reactions — plus read receipts, disappearing messages, and chat streaks, with optional Gemini-powered translation and auto-replies (configurable mood). High-quality voice and video calls include per-minute billing options so hosts and creators can earn from every minute.

Social: Stories, Feed & Reels

A complete social layer keeps members coming back daily. Stories support photos, video, music, highlights, sponsorships, and paid private views. The feed supports multi-media posts, hashtags, sponsored content, and subscriber-only visibility. Short-video reels add trending discovery, search, saves, and pay-per-view unlocks.

Live Streaming & Voice Rooms

Anyone can go live in one tap with thumbnails, goals, viewer counts, comments, gifts, and co-stream invites. Run PK battles between streamers, offer private entry-fee streams, and notify followers automatically. Scheduled public voice rooms add a Clubhouse-style layer with per-minute earnings for hosts.

Interactive Dating Games

Six ready-to-play experiences set Lamat apart from standard swipe apps — each with coin entry options, subscriber or verification gates, and configurable themes and rewards:

  • Blind Date Roulette — anonymous voice matching with icebreakers and optional reveal.
  • Hot Takes — AI-assisted speed dating: generated questions, smart matching, and timed chats with voice/video and rewards.
  • Double Date — group dating with lobbies, group chat, calls, and date planning.
  • Pop the Balloon — live game-show format with rounds, audience chat, and gifts.
  • Echoes — missed-connections feed based on real-world proximity.
  • Live Mingle — real-time queue for discovering who is nearby right now.

Events, Meetups & Community

Bring dating offline with in-person events, date-meeting requests with budgets and statuses, and contests with prizes. Follows, profile-visit tracking, blocking, and reporting round out a healthy community with built-in moderation tools.

Monetization & Payments

Multiple revenue streams work out of the box: coin packages with in-app purchases, Pro subscription tiers, profile boosts, virtual gifts, pay-per-view stories, feeds and reels, creator subscriptions, referrals, and coin-to-cash withdrawals with admin approval. Payments are handled through 7 gateways — Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay, Mercado Pago, Mollie, and Flutterwave — with webhooks, multi-currency support, and hosted checkout.

Admin Panel

Run the whole platform from the browser. The analytics dashboard shows revenue, user growth, content activity, engagement, and moderation queues (reports, withdrawals, referrals) at a glance, with recent users and transactions alongside.

Manage users, photos, feeds, blogs, music, themes, languages, coin packages, Pro plans, gifts, products, contests, events, and push campaigns (OneSignal + Firebase) from one place, with moderator roles for your team and a visual theme editor with live preview plus dark mode.

Over 50 no-code setting groups cover General, Swipe, Chat, Calls, Live, Stories, Feed, Reels, Voice Rooms, each dating game, Events, Boost, Rewards, AI Responder, Verification, Fake Users, Watermark, Nudity Detection, Giphy, Rankings, Streaks, Referrals, Ecommerce, Withdrawals, Social Login, Email, Payments, Pusher, Analytics, Mobile Apps, Ads, Genders, Interests, and Profile Questions — so limits, prices, and feature toggles never need code changes.

AI, Safety & Engagement

Where it helps, AI lends a hand: Gemini can handle translation, auto-replies, Hot Takes matching, and conversation insights; Sight Engine screens uploads for nudity; and optional AI-driven fake-user activity helps seed the community at launch. Photos can carry watermarks and admin approval, and auth is protected by Sanctum tokens, email verification, rate limiting, and granular moderator permissions. Rankings, photo-border unlocks, chat and login streaks, and leaderboards keep engagement high — all tunable from the admin panel without code.

Requirements:

Setup, Technology & Requirements

Lamat ships with a 4-step local web installer (requirements check, database setup, admin account, completion) plus an auto-install helper for production. The installer runs fully on your own server and performs no purchase-code or online license check. The stack is Laravel + Livewire + Alpine.js + Bootstrap 5 with a Sanctum REST API, Pusher real-time events, Zego Cloud audio/video, Intervention Image processing, and Giphy, Google Maps, and Geolocation integrations.

  • PHP 8.1+, MySQL 5.7+ / MariaDB, Composer, Apache (mod_rewrite) or Nginx
  • Flutter 3.47 stable or higher (tested with 3.47.2) with Dart 3.12 or higher
  • Flutter app for Android, iOS, and Web with deep links and push via FCM + OneSignal
  • Required PHP extensions: BCMath, Ctype, Fileinfo, JSON, Mbstring, OpenSSL, PDO, Tokenizer, XML, GD, cURL, Exif, Imagick, intl

Paid 3rd Party Services Required

These external services may require their own paid subscriptions or API keys to enable certain features. Some are optional, some core features require them.

  • Google Gemini API - Required for AI auto-responder, chat auto-translate, Hot Takes AI question/topic generation, AI matching, and AI conversation analysis. Free tier available with limits.
  • Zego Cloud - Required for live streaming, voice chat rooms, voice/video calls. Provides the real-time audio/video infrastructure. Paid plan required for production.
  • Pusher - Required for real-time broadcasting (chat delivery, live events, presence channels). Free tier available with limited connections.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) - Required for push notifications on mobile devices. Free to use.
  • OneSignal - Required for marketing push notifications and cross-platform push delivery. Free tier available with limited subscribers.
  • Google Geolocation API - Required for location-based features, city search, and geocoding. Free tier available with monthly credit.
  • Google Maps API - Required for profile map display and location features. Free tier available.
  • Stripe - Payment gateway for credit/debit card payments. Transaction-based fees.
  • PayPal - Payment gateway for PayPal payments. Transaction-based fees.
  • Paystack - Payment gateway for African markets. Transaction-based fees.
  • Razorpay - Payment gateway for Indian market. Transaction-based fees.
  • Mercado Pago - Payment gateway for Latin American markets. Transaction-based fees.
  • Mollie - Payment gateway for European markets. Transaction-based fees.
  • Flutterwave - Payment gateway for African markets. Transaction-based fees.
  • Giphy API - Required for GIF support in chat. Free tier available.
  • Sight Engine - Required for automatic nudity detection on uploaded photos. Paid plan required.
  • Google OAuth / Apple OAuth / Facebook OAuth - Required for social login features. Free to use with developer account.
  • SMTP Email Service - Required for email notifications (welcome, match, like, chat, password reset). Services like Mailjet, SendGrid, or your own SMTP server.
  • Google Analytics - Optional website analytics tracking. Free to use.
